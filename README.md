# 📊 Trader Behavior Insights: Impact of Market Sentiment

### 🚀 Sentiment-Driven Analysis of Crypto Trading Behavior using Data Science

> An advanced data analytics project focused on understanding how Bitcoin market sentiment (Fear vs Greed) influences trader profitability, leverage usage, risk-taking behavior, and trading decisions using real historical trading data from Hyperliquid.

---

## 🌟 Project Overview

Financial markets are heavily influenced by **human psychology and sentiment**.
This project explores the relationship between **Bitcoin Fear & Greed sentiment** and real-world trader behavior to uncover patterns that impact:

📈 Profitability (PnL)
⚖️ Risk Exposure & Leverage Usage
🔄 Long vs Short Trading Decisions
📊 Position Sizing Behavior

Using data-driven analysis, the project provides actionable insights into how emotions and market sentiment affect decision-making in volatile crypto markets.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

✅ Analyze how market sentiment impacts trading performance
✅ Study leverage behavior during Fear & Greed phases
✅ Identify patterns in profitable vs loss-making trades
✅ Discover behavioral biases in crypto trading
✅ Build sentiment-aware trading insights for risk management

## 🔥 Key Insights

### 😨 Fear Markets

✅ Traders showed more cautious behavior
✅ Lower leverage usage
✅ More stable and consistent returns

---

### 🤑 Greed Markets

⚠️ Increased over-leveraging behavior
⚠️ Higher risk exposure
⚠️ Larger average losses due to aggressive trading

---

### 📌 Behavioral Patterns Observed

🔹 Overconfidence during Greed sentiment
🔹 Risk-averse decisions during Fear phases
🔹 Strong emotional influence on trading strategies
🔹 High leverage strongly correlated with larger drawdowns
